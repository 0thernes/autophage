**Epistemic Tiers:**
- `kernel-checked` — Machine-checked mathematical theorem verified in Pure Lean 4.35.0 across the reference kernel and independent proof checkers (`0` `sorry` in solution, `0` `native_decide`/`bv_decide`, axioms strictly `⊆ {propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}`).
- `program-audited` — Deterministically verified by compiled C11 assertions, planted negative mutants, differential oracle checks, cryptographic hashes, or Python property tests on live hardware.
- `empirical` — Measured evolutionary or statistical phenomenon observed across locked seeds (`5000..5023`) on a finite `32×32` or `16×16` torus; reproducible on the same code/seeds, not a universal law of biology or chemistry.
- `indicator-only` — Heuristic composite score (`OVI`, `OVBS-52`, `BedauPackard_Anew`, `Assembly`, `Krakauer_I`, `MarkovBlanket`) useful as an internal comparative barometer, not an established physical constant.
- `open-conjecture` — Overarching scientific/mathematical problem in `0thernes/unsolved-problems-mega-atlas` that remains **OPEN** (`0/1,900` retired).

## Scope notes (2026-11-26 statement audit & v1.5.0 remediation)

- **What Lean proves (C1):** for the repository's Lean models (`lean/AutophageProofs/Spec.lean`), under a trial-division irreducibility test (divisors of degree 1–4), exactly 30 of the 256 polynomials x⁸+p over F₂ pass, and they match the 30-entry table in `src/galois_math.c` (`T12a`); for each, the 2×2 Cauchy code `[[1,3],[5,1]]` recovers both data bytes from the two parity bytes (`T16`); affine GF(2) maps with linear inverses round-trip (`T05`, `T05a`); Fredkin conserves XOR, AND and `popcnt64` (`T19`, `T20`, `T20a`) while Toffoli does not conserve `popcnt64` (`AT01`); masked-XOR kinship is transitive (`T25`, `T25a`); multi-step integer ledgers keep a zero residual (`T29a`, witness `T45`) and never decrease `e_heat_snk` under non-negative heat radiation while `oviGate 0 raw_ovi = 0` (`T30`, with unguarded/negative refutation `AT04`); and 8 adversarial refutations (`AT01`–`AT08`) witness sharp failure boundaries.
- **Prior work:** the count of 30 irreducible binary polynomials of degree 8, the involutivity of Toffoli and Fredkin gates, and the invertibility of a 2×2 matrix with nonzero determinant are textbook results. The contribution is a mechanised check against this codebase's definitions.
- **Not proved in Lean:** JIT machine-code emission, C11 memory safety and FP64 drift (tests only; see `LIMITATIONS.md` sec. 2).
- **Kernel replay:** six-kernel acceptance (`lake check --paranoid`, `lake comparator --paranoid`) requires Linux `bubblewrap` and runs in Ubuntu CI; on Windows the Lean reference kernel (`lean4`, `leanchecker`, `leanchecker-paranoid`) plus three independent Rust checkers (`nanoda_bin`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`) run locally.
