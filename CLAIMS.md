Every claim made in `README.md`, `VERIFICATION.md`, `docs/`, and `paper/main.tex` is indexed below with an explicit **epistemic tier**, exact **verification command**, **artifact**, and **refutation condition (what would prove us wrong)**.

**Epistemic Tiers:**
- `kernel-checked` — Machine-checked mathematical theorem verified in Pure Lean 4.35.0 across independent proof kernels (`0` `sorry` in solution, `0` `native_decide`/`bv_decide`, axioms strictly `⊆ {propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}`).
- `program-audited` — Deterministically verified by compiled C11 assertions, planted negative mutants, differential oracle checks, cryptographic hashes, or Python property tests on live hardware.
- `empirical` — Measured evolutionary or statistical phenomenon observed across locked seeds (`5000..5023`) on a finite `32×32` or `16×16` torus; reproducible on the same code/seeds, not a universal law of biology or chemistry.
- `indicator-only` — Heuristic composite score (`OVI`, `OVBS-52`, `BedauPackard_Anew`, `Assembly`, `Krakauer_I`, `MarkovBlanket`) useful as an internal comparative barometer, not an established physical constant.
- `open-conjecture` — Overarching scientific/mathematical problem in `0thernes/unsolved-problems-mega-atlas` that remains **OPEN** (`0/1,900` retired).
