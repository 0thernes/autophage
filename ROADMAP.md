- **Claiming retirement of open biological abiogenesis (`BIO-001`) or general OEE (`AI-045`):** Digital abiogenesis in a finite 256-byte x86-64 substrate is an empirical testbed, never a substitute for wet-lab origin-of-life chemistry.
